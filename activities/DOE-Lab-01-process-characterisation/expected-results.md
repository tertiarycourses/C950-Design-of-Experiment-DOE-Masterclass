## Minimum acceptance criteria

- The factor table, four treatment combinations, response definition, random run order and go/no-go rule are all present.
- The NovaDOE export matches the factor names and run count in the workbook.
- No response value is silently discarded or changed.
- The conclusion distinguishes statistical evidence, engineering importance and limitations.
- A follow-up or confirmation action has an owner and measurable acceptance condition.
